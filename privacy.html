Media Naranja

Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026

Media Naranja is a habit app for couples. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

  • No ads, and we never sell your data.
  • Your partner sees only what the app is built to show them. Some things stay private even from them.
  • Picante answers you say "no" to are never shown to anyone.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the app or on the web.

Who we are

Media Naranja is run from Belize. Questions or requests about your data: medianaranja.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account

You sign in with Google. We receive your email address and an identifier for your Google account. We never see your Google password.

Your profile

  • The name or nickname you choose, and a photo if you add one.
  • Your birthday, used only to check your age. It is never shown to your partner.
  • Your time zone and language, so days, reminders and streaks follow your clock.
  • Your colour, quiet hours, and notification and privacy settings.

Private to you

The goals you pick and the foods you don't eat stay private, even from your partner. We use them only to suggest habits and keep foods out of your deck.

Shared with your partner

Media Naranja is built for two, so this is visible to both of you:

  • Habits and check-ins, including check-in photos. "Mine" habits you choose to hide stay hidden.
  • Streaks, nudges, cheers, reactions, the weekly challenge and any bet you set.
  • Food and date-idea matches, the shopping list, the Date Jar, important dates and your shared photo album. Your individual likes are not shown, only matches and a daily count.

Picante (optional, adults only)

Picante is a set of questions and games for couples. It is off unless you are both 18 or older and you both turn it on. We store your answers to its cards. Your partner only sees a card when you both said "yes", or one of you said "yes" and the other "maybe". A "no" is never shown. Picante answers are deleted immediately if either of you turns it off or you unlink. Notifications never mention it, and it is never used for analytics.

Device and technical information

  • A push token for this phone, if you allow notifications.
  • Crash reports (app version, device model, operating system and what went wrong), so we can fix bugs.
  • Usage events, such as "a habit was checked in", linked to a random internal ID. They never include your name, email, habit names, messages, photos or anything from Picante. You can turn this off in Profile → Privacy.

Feedback you send

The message you write, plus the app version and phone platform.

How we use it

  • To run the app and keep you and your partner in sync.
  • To send the notifications you allow, respecting your quiet hours.
  • To keep accounts secure, fix crashes and improve the app.

We use your information to provide the service you signed up for. For sensitive, optional features like Picante, we rely on your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time by turning the feature off.

Who helps us run the app

These providers process data on our behalf, only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in (servers in the United States).
  • Google: sign-in, and Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications.
  • Expo: sends push notifications to your phone.
  • PostHog: anonymous usage analytics, which you can turn off (United States).
  • Sentry: crash reports (United States).

We don't sell or rent your data, show ads, or share it with data brokers. We would only disclose information if the law required it.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored and processed in the United States by the providers above, who protect it with industry-standard security.

How long we keep it

  • For as long as you have an account.
  • The activity feed shows the last 30 days. Records of sent notifications are kept for 30 days.
  • If you unlink from your partner, Picante answers and matches are deleted immediately for both of you. Food and date matches and the shopping list are removed, and each of you keeps your own history. Re-linking with the same person within 30 days restores your shared history, but not Picante.
  • If you delete your account, there is a 30-day grace period (signing back in cancels it). Then your profile, photos and everything that is only yours are permanently deleted. Your partner keeps their own history. Feedback you sent is kept without any link to you. Copies in our providers' backups are overwritten within a short time.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your information, get a copy of it, withdraw consent, or object to how we use it. You can do most of this yourself in the app (Profile and its settings). For anything else, email medianaranja.app@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, and we'll reply within 30 days. We follow Belize's Data Protection Act 2021 and apply its standards to everyone who uses the app.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules make sure only you, and where intended your partner, can read your couple's data. Nobody can read your Picante "no" answers through the app or its servers' interfaces.

Age

Media Naranja is for people 16 and older; Picante is for adults 18 and older. If we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For significant changes, we'll also tell you in the app.
