Delete your account

You can delete your Media Naranja account and its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Media Naranja and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Hold the button to confirm.

Without the app

Email medianaranja.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". Send it from the email address of the Google account you use with Media Naranja, so we know it's you. We'll confirm, and delete your account within 30 days.

What happens

  • Your partner is unlinked right away.
  • For 30 days your account can still be recovered: signing back in cancels the deletion.
  • After 30 days, these are permanently deleted: your profile and photo, your habits, check-ins and check-in photos, your messages and reactions, your swipes and Picante answers, your notification settings and push tokens, and photos you added to the shared album.

What stays

  • Your partner keeps their own history, including their copies of habits you shared.
  • Feedback you sent is kept without any link to you.
  • Copies in our providers' backups are overwritten within a short time.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
